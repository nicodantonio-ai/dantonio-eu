PRIVACY & COOKIES

Privacy by design,
kept deliberately simple.

This website is designed to minimise data collection. It does not use advertising, profiling or analytics cookies.

Last updated: 9 September 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller for this website is Domenico D’Antonio, Latina, Italy · P.IVA 01896420591. For privacy-related requests, you can write to contact@dantonio.eu.

2. Data processed through this website

The site itself does not include registration forms, newsletters, advertising trackers or analytics tools. Personal data may be processed only when you choose to contact me by email or use an external service linked from this website, such as the appointment-booking page.

Email

If you contact me by email, the information you provide is used to reply to your message and manage the related professional correspondence.

Appointment booking

The “Book a conversation” link opens a Google Calendar appointment page. Information submitted there is processed through Google’s service and according to the privacy terms applicable to that service. dantonio.eu does not receive that data through a form embedded on this website.

3. Legal bases and purposes

Depending on the interaction, personal data may be processed to respond to your request, take steps at your request before entering into a professional relationship, perform contractual or professional activities, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Where appropriate, processing may also rely on legitimate interests connected with managing professional communications and protecting the website.

4. Hosting and technical logs

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, technical infrastructure may process information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, browser information and server logs for security, reliability and service operation. Such processing is managed by the relevant service provider under its own terms and policies.

5. Cookies

No consent banner is currently shown because dantonio.eu does not intentionally use advertising, profiling or analytics cookies.

The website is intentionally kept lightweight and does not embed third-party advertising or analytics technologies. If future versions introduce tools that require consent under applicable law, this notice and the site’s consent mechanism will be updated before those tools are activated.

External websites opened through links — for example Google Calendar or LinkedIn — may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Those technologies are outside dantonio.eu once you leave this website.

6. Data retention

Personal data received through direct professional communications is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including any applicable contractual, administrative, accounting or legal requirements.

7. Recipients and international services

Data may be processed by service providers used for email, hosting, calendar scheduling or other necessary technical functions. Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area subject to the safeguards made available under applicable data-protection law.

8. Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, or object to certain processing, subject to the conditions provided by law. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, including the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

9. External links

This website contains links to external services and websites. Once you follow an external link, the privacy practices of the destination service apply.

10. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website, its services or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

This page is intended as a practical transparency notice for the current minimal, non-tracking version of dantonio.eu. It should be reviewed if new forms, analytics, embedded media, advertising or other third-party technologies are added.
